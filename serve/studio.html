Make a song

Pick a style, edit the words, press create. One model is loaded at a time and takes run one after another; choosing another model switches it.

The song's length follows the lyrics — more sections, longer song. It can run past ten minutes on one card.
The best vocals here, and the slowest: it takes all three cards, one song at a time, about 7 seconds of making per second of song (2-minute song ≈ 14 min; the longest tested, 3 min 37 s, took 25 min). Everything else waits while it runs. The song may end before the length you set.
Instrumental only, and it stops at 2 minutes — the model's hard limit. About 8 seconds to make, whatever the length.
Advanced settings
Same seed + same settings = the exact same song. Leave blank for a new one each time; the seed used is always shown so a good take can be remade.
